# Qurotema

*Qurotema* is a first person music exploration game with puzzle elements.

Di, a remote operator, is tasked with investigating Qurotema, an otherworldly universe hidden from standard voidspace navigation systems.

---

Qurotema is currently a work in progress, nearing completion as of May 2026.

What remains to be finished:

- The integration of the audio assets from an audio overhaul.
- Further audio integration into player-driven systems, like movement.
- Expansion of the late-game story.

---

Qurotema is made using Unity and its high definition rendering pipeline (HDRP).

It features a dynamic soundtrack with an internal clock designed in FMOD, produced by myself and with the support of talented friend and composer [Sanea Ima](https://saneaima.com).

The soundtrack evolves (changing chord progression, triggering audio events, moving filter parameters) based on nearly every single player action. From walking, to interacting with the musical instruments in the world, the player produces the soundtrack to their experience. They can actively choose how their music evolves through deliberate engagement with game systems.

The game also includes:

- Musical instruments in the world to interact with in more musically traditional ways.
- A story that elaborates on the nature and history of Qurotema and its underlying mystery.
- A constructed language, [i-tema](https://v-os.ca/i-tema), which provides a peek into the secrets of the hidden universe.
- A custom character controller with its own physics to create satisfying silky movement across Qurotema's oily landscapes.
- Various reactive shaders and VFX across the world for rich UX feedback and unique visuals.
- A weighty stylized UI integrated with Qurotema's different game systems.
